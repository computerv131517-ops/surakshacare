MEMBERSHIP AGREEMENT

SurakshaCare Membership Terms & Conditions

Please review these terms before activating your membership. They explain membership validity, service coverage, customer responsibilities, payments, cancellations and limitations.

1. Membership Eligibility & Validity

Membership is available to eligible customers who provide accurate account, contact and registered-device information. Unless otherwise stated in the selected plan, membership remains valid for twelve (12) months from the confirmed activation date.

2. Plan Benefits & Registered Devices

Benefits are limited to the services, checkups, support levels and device coverage specifically included in the selected membership plan. Only devices properly registered to the customer's account are eligible for applicable membership benefits. Customers must provide accurate make, model and other requested details.

3. Preventive Checkups

The number of preventive checkups is limited to the allowance shown on the selected plan. Checkups are subject to appointment availability and service-area coverage. Unused checkups expire with the membership and are not automatically carried forward unless expressly stated otherwise.

4. Technician & Service Support

Technician visits are subject to service availability, location, operating hours and appointment slots. Priority or instant support does not guarantee a specific arrival time. Emergency or out-of-plan services may attract additional charges, which will be communicated before chargeable work is undertaken where reasonably possible.

5. Repairs, Parts & Replacement

Membership is a preventive-care and service program and does not automatically provide unlimited free repairs, spare parts or product replacement. Parts, major repairs, consumables, third-party services and replacement costs may be chargeable according to the applicable service terms and will be communicated to the customer before approval wherever practicable.

6. Pre-existing Conditions & Misuse

Faults, damage or conditions that existed before membership activation may be excluded from membership benefits. Damage caused by misuse, negligence, unauthorized modification, accident, improper installation, abnormal use or intentional damage may also be excluded.

7. Customer Responsibilities

The customer must provide safe and reasonable access to the registered device, provide accurate information, follow recommended maintenance instructions and cooperate with service personnel. Customers remain responsible for protecting personal belongings and data before a device is serviced.

8. Pricing, Payments & Taxes

Membership fees are payable according to the price displayed at the time of purchase. Applicable taxes or additional service charges, where relevant, will be communicated separately. Membership activation is subject to successful payment confirmation. A receipt or invoice may be issued for completed transactions.

9. Cancellation, Refunds & Renewal

Cancellation and refund eligibility will depend on the applicable plan and the stage at which benefits or services have been used. Approved refunds, where applicable, may be subject to reasonable processing adjustments. Membership does not automatically renew unless an explicit auto-renewal option has been enabled and accepted by the customer.

10. Service Delays & Unavoidable Events

Service timelines may be affected by technician availability, weather, transport disruption, public emergencies, parts availability, power conditions or other circumstances beyond reasonable operational control. SurakshaCare will make reasonable efforts to communicate material delays.

11. Fraud, Misuse & Suspension

Providing false information, submitting fraudulent claims, manipulating service records or deliberately misusing membership benefits may result in suspension or termination of membership, subject to applicable law.

12. Privacy & Service Records

Customer information may be processed for account management, service delivery, scheduling, support, billing and service-history purposes in accordance with the applicable privacy policy. Relevant information may be shared with authorized technicians or service partners where necessary to provide the requested service.

13. Changes to Membership Terms

SurakshaCare may update plan benefits, pricing or these terms from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through appropriate channels where required. The terms applicable to a membership will be identified at the time of activation, subject to lawful updates.

14. Governing & Applicable Law

These terms are intended as the customer-facing membership rules for the SurakshaCare service. Any formal commercial agreement will be subject to applicable laws and regulations of India and the applicable jurisdiction.

15. Customer Consent

By selecting “I Agree & Continue”, the customer confirms that they have read and understood these membership terms and agree to the applicable plan conditions.

For the SIH/demo version, membership data is stored locally in the browser. A production launch should connect these terms and consent records to the backend and applicable legal/privacy documents.
